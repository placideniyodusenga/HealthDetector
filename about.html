About Our Student Fainting Detection System

Revolutionizing student health monitoring in Rwanda with cutting-edge wearable technology and real-time alert systems

Our Student Health Monitoring System is designed to track and analyze vital signs like temperature, oxygen level (SpO2), and heart rate in real-time. It helps schools maintain a healthy environment and respond promptly to any health issues, with special focus on detecting fainting episodes before they occur.

Our Mission

To protect student health across Rwanda by providing early warning systems for fainting episodes and other medical emergencies through innovative wearable technology and real-time monitoring.

Our Vision

To create a future where no student's health emergency goes unnoticed, ensuring every child in Rwanda can learn in a safe, monitored environment with immediate medical response capabilities.

Why Choose Our System?

Real-time Monitoring

Continuous tracking of heart rate, SpO2, temperature, and movement patterns

Instant Alerts

Immediate notifications to nurses and teachers when abnormal readings are detected

GPS Tracking

Predictive Analytics

AI-powered predictions to identify students at risk of fainting
