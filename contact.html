Frequently Asked Questions

How fast is your response?

We respond to all inquiries within 24 hours on weekdays. Emergency support is available 24/7 for schools with active systems.

Do you deliver nationwide?

Yes! We install and support our fainting detection systems in schools across all provinces of Rwanda.

What about maintenance?

We provide regular maintenance and 24/7 technical support for all installed systems at no additional cost.

Is there a demo available?

Absolutely! Contact us to schedule a free demonstration at your school with a 7-day trial period.

Do you train our staff?

Yes, we provide comprehensive training for teachers, nurses, and administrators on system usage.

Is student data secure?

All health data is encrypted and stored securely in compliance with Rwandan data protection laws.
